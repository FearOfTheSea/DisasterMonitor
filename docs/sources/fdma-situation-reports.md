# FDMA situation reports

Official FDMA casualty, damage, infrastructure, and response evidence for selected
Japan earthquakes. The bounded index and newest matching HTML/PDF report require no
configuration. Sanitized extractable text and recognized facts become attributed
claims. Event time is converted to Japan time before matching the Japan-calendar report
date; publication dates are constructed in Japan time then normalized to UTC. Unreadable
PDFs, malformed indexes, and transport failures are diagnostics; no report yields
partial evidence. OCR is absent and only maintained patterns/layouts are supported.
Accuracy, latency, and availability guarantees are unknown. Tests: FDMA adapter,
timezone-boundary, PDF, and workflow suites.
